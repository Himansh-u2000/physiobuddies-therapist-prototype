Common questions

How do I start a session using OTP?
Navigate to the appointment, tap "Start session", then ask the patient for the 6-digit OTP shown on their Physiobuddies app. Enter it to unlock the active session screen. The OTP expires in 10 minutes.
When are payouts processed?
Payouts are processed every Monday for sessions completed the previous week (Monday–Sunday). Funds typically reach your bank account within 1–2 business days. You can track all pending and settled amounts in the Earnings section.
Why was my document rejected?
Documents are rejected when they are blurry, incomplete, expired, or the text is not clearly legible. Check the rejection reason in Verification → Documents and re-upload a clear, high-resolution scan (minimum 300 DPI). JPEG, PNG, or PDF formats are accepted.
How do I update my availability?
Go to Profile → Availability. You can set weekly working hours, block specific dates (e.g. holidays), and differentiate between clinic and home visit slots. Changes take effect immediately on your public profile.
What happens if a patient cancels last minute?
Cancellations within 2 hours of the session time are subject to the platform's cancellation policy. If the patient cancels within 1 hour, you may be eligible for a 50% session fee as compensation. Contact support if you believe you were not compensated correctly.
